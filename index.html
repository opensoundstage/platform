04 / THE PRINCIPLES

Keep it simple.
Keep it yours.

One core

New abilities build on the same four commands. No new language versions required.

BUILDs build on BUILDs

A BUILD can execute another BUILD. Creations become pieces of larger creations.

DO is reusable

Execute the same BUILD repeatedly. The saved instructions remain unchanged.

No hidden PLAY

PLAY, COMPARE, and DECIDE are not magic commands. Their behavior must be defined.

Artist ownership

Keep your work. Share it voluntarily. Reuse respects the creator's permission.

Honest engineering

BUILD is a proposed language, not a finished interpreter. Identity, conditions, and playback need design.

04 / THE IDEA
