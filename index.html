01 / THE IDEA

The platform belongs to the music.

OpenSoundstage is being built as an open-source, API-first foundation for the independent music world.

Artists can create and connect. Labels can participate without giving up their identity. Communities can build on shared tools instead of starting from zero.

We're at the beginning. This is our direction, not a promise that every feature already exists.

02 / WHAT WE BELIEVE

01

Music first.

Everything we build should help music and the people behind it.

02

Open by design.

Public code, interoperable tools, and APIs that invite new ideas.

03

Independent together.

Artists, labels, and communities can connect without becoming the same organization.
